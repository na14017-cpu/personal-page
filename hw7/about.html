About

Overview

MercyMed is a private medical centre in Bishkek providing high-quality care based on modern medical knowledge and international experience. We are an outpatient clinic: consultations, medical procedures, ultrasound, medications and infusions, with no overnight stays.

History

Our mobile clinics have served different regions for over 10 years. The clinic in Bishkek opened in October 2023.

How we work

Compassion, holistic care, integrity and trust, delivered by competent, locally trained professionals.
